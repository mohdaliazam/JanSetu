# JanSetu project brief

## Product outcome

Help a district planning officer turn fragmented multilingual development requests into traceable proposed infrastructure projects. Citizens describe a need; JanSetu organises reports, joins them to local service gaps and planned investments, and presents explainable priorities for review.

Track: AI for Digital Public Infrastructure & Governance. Theme: Innovation. India is the demonstrated deployment context; BRICS interoperability is an architectural direction, not a claim of cross-country deployment.

## Users

- Citizen: submits a text or voice development request in a supported language and sees what was understood.
- Planning analyst: investigates grouped needs, missing evidence, existing plans, and ranked project candidates.
- Judge: can inspect a complete demo without supplying personal information or gaining administrative privileges in a real civic system.

## Signature demonstration

A Hindi water-service report in synthetic locality IN-BR-PAT-L01 becomes a structured record. An English report about the same need joins its group. The analyst sees the local service gap, population context, and existing plan status. JanSetu generates a cited project brief with transparent score components. A new report changes the group evidence and may change its score; no artificial rank jump is promised.

## Value and limits

The product supports planning; it does not approve budgets, verify allegations, estimate engineering costs without evidence, guarantee project impact, or handle emergency dispatch. Reported urgency and synthetic indicators are not verified conditions. A report acknowledgement is not an official government ticket.

## Data commitments

Use original synthetic records for the prototype, labelled on all relevant screens and exports. Every indicator and plan has provenance and a date. Missing data remains missing, not zero. Original meaning and language are preserved alongside redacted display text and AI interpretation. Avoid collecting names, phone numbers, identity numbers, or precise homes.

## Success signals to measure

Working Hindi/English flows; extraction correctness on the curated cases; no cross-locality grouping; auditable score arithmetic; linked source IDs in briefs; isolation between demo users; persisted requests after refresh and process restart; truthful live/fixture AI badges; deployed end-to-end playback. Record actual measurements, not invented percentages or user impact.
